# Ownlane pricing

## Pricing principle

Ownlane does **not** take a service fee or commission from what creators sell, earn in tips, receive as gifts, or make through bookings.

> Creators keep 100% of their earnings.

Payment processing is shown transparently at checkout. Depending on a creator's choice and the payment method, the buyer can pay it, or the creator can absorb it. Ownlane must never disguise a platform commission as a payout, withdrawal, or service deduction.

Ownlane makes money through subscriptions and optional usage-based add-ons—not by taxing a creator's success.

## Plans

Prices below are placeholders until market, operating costs, and payment-provider economics are confirmed.

| Plan | Intended for | Indicative price | Includes |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Free | New creators establishing their business home | $0/month | One Ownlane page, core branding, social links, limited offers/products, tips and gifts, basic analytics, standard checkout |
| Pro | Active independent creators | $19/month | Unlimited offers/products, digital fulfilment, bookings, custom domain, advanced page design, email capture, audience CRM, detailed analytics |
| Business | Growing creator brands and small teams | $49/month | Everything in Pro, team access, memberships/subscriptions, affiliates, advanced automations, integrations/webhooks, branded checkout, priority support |

Annual billing should offer a meaningful discount, for example two months free.

## Optional add-ons

Add-ons let creators buy additional capability without a hidden commission:

- Extra digital-file and video hosting
- Transactional email or SMS volume beyond a plan allowance
- AI storefront assistant and AI copy/product tools
- Premium storefront templates
- Advanced automation or integration capacity
- Additional team seats

## Checkout transparency

Every payment screen should clearly state:

- Offer price
- Payment processing charge, if applicable
- Whether the creator or buyer is covering that charge
- Total paid by the buyer
- Amount the creator receives

The phrase “100% of your earnings” must remain literally true in product behaviour and marketing.
