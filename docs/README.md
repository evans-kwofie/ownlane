# Ownlane

**Your brand. Your audience. Your business.**

Ownlane is a unified commerce home for internet-native creators. It gives every kind of creator one branded place to share their work, sell what they make, receive support, offer services, and build a direct relationship with their audience.

This folder captures the initial product and brand direction.

## Documents

- [Product vision](product-vision.md) — problem, positioning, audience, product pillars, and launch scope.
- [Pricing](pricing.md) — creator-first, zero-commission pricing model and plan definitions.
- [Brand](brand.md) — name rationale, message, and early voice guidelines.
- [Repository architecture](architecture.md) — monorepo layout and tooling decisions.
- [Target customers](target-customers.md) — customer segments, interview priorities, and discovery guide.

## Core promise

One link. Every way your audience can support your business.
